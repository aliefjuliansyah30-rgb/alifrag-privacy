AliFrag — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

AliFrag ("the Bot") is a Discord application designed to provide gaming and community-related features.

1. Information We Collect

AliFrag may process information necessary to provide its features, including:

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord Server ID
  • Discord username and display name
  • Commands and interactions sent to the Bot
  • Steam ID or Steam profile information when voluntarily provided by a user

AliFrag does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by AliFrag is used only to:

  • Provide and operate Bot features
  • Respond to user commands
  • Display requested gaming or community information
  • Maintain and improve the Bot
  • Prevent abuse and technical problems

3. Data Sharing

AliFrag does not sell or rent user information. Information may be sent to third-party services only when necessary to provide a requested feature, such as retrieving publicly available Steam or gaming information.

4. Data Storage

AliFrag only stores information when it is necessary for the operation of a feature. We do not intentionally retain personal information longer than necessary.

5. User Requests

If you want information associated with your use of AliFrag to be removed, you may contact the Bot administrator.

6. Third-Party Services

AliFrag may interact with Discord, Steam, or other third-party services. Their respective privacy policies and terms apply to information handled by those services.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AliFrag's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the AliFrag administrator through the Discord server where the Bot is used.
